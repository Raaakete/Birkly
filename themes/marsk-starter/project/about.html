Principles

How this journal works

01

Slowly

One entry most Sundays. Sometimes none. Rushing a journal about slowness would be embarrassing.

02

Honestly

Failed loaves and abandoned projects get written up with the same care as the successes.

03

Quietly

No trackers, no pop-ups, no urgency. Just text, and the occasional picture of bread.
