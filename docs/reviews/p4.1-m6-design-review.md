## Summary

Connector-first information architecture meets Phase 4.1 locked decisions: primary “Connect your AI app” with Connector URL + OAuth flow, secondary built-in assistant one-screen quick connect, HTTPS warning on non-public sites, approval queue in floating chat (badge + tab), and `mcp-bridge.js` only under collapsed Developer section.

## Critical fixes applied

1. Renamed connection pill from “OAuth connector” to “App connection”.
2. Dev section copy avoids stdio/bridge jargon.
3. FAB badge class separated from in-card status pill (`ai-chat-fab-badge` vs `ai-chat-approval-status-pill`).

## Improvements applied

- Stronger secondary section styling (muted background).
- Chat tabs sync `aria-selected`.
- FAB `aria-label` includes pending approval count.
- `.tip-box-warning` defined for HTTPS banner.
