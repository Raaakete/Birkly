# 12 — Risks & Mitigations

## Product & technology

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Pre-beta defects in production | Medium | High | 156+ test suites, P28 journey automation, qa-verifier gates |
| Studio complexity overwhelms SMB users | Medium | Medium | Progressive UX, templates, agency-led onboarding |
| Security incident in core or plugin | Low–Med | High | Security-by-default, QC pipeline, 2FA, audits **[TBD budget]** |
| Plugin supply-chain issue | Medium | High | Marketplace manifest schema, QC, signed artifacts |
