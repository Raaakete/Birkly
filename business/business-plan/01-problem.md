# 01 — Problem

## The CMS market forces a bad trade-off

Teams that manage websites and digital content today face a recurring dilemma:

1. **SaaS CMS platforms** (Contentful, Sanity, Storyblok, etc.) optimize for speed to launch but create **vendor lock-in**: content models, workflows, and billing live inside a third-party boundary. Costs scale with seats, API calls, and assets. Exit means migration projects measured in months.

2. **Self-hosted open platforms** (WordPress, Drupal) offer ownership but often bring **complexity debt**: plugin conflicts, security surface area, database maintenance, and upgrade cycles that break production sites.

3. **Developer-first headless stacks** (Strapi, Directus) improve API ergonomics but frequently depend on **npm/Node build chains**, container orchestration, and ongoing DevOps — raising the bar for agencies and in-house teams who want “upload and run.”

## Who feels this most

| Segment | Pain |
|---------|------|
| **Agencies & studios** | Repeatable client stacks without per-project framework roulette; predictable hosting and handoff. |
| **SMB owners** | Control over content and costs without surprise subscriptions or “call the developer for every typo.” |
| **Developers** | API-first content, portable data, extensibility without shipping a second application runtime. |
| **AI-forward teams** | Structured content and approval workflows — not opaque SaaS black boxes. |

## Why incumbents leave a gap

- **WordPress** remains dominant but carries legacy architecture and security expectations that many greenfield projects want to avoid.
- **Headless SaaS** wins on DX for startups funded to absorb recurring fees; it is a poor fit for cost-sensitive SMBs and sovereignty-conscious EU buyers.
- **“Zero-dependency” self-hosted CMS** options are rare at production quality with a modern admin, marketplace, and commerce story.

## Opportunity statement

There is room for a **self-hosted, flat-file CMS** that is simple enough for non-developers, precise enough for developers, and **economically aligned** with customers (no mandatory SaaS rent) — with a **curated extension marketplace** for revenue without compromising core portability.
