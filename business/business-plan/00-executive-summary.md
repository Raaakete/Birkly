# 00 — Executive Summary

**Birkly** is a self-hosted, flat-file content management **platform** for teams that refuse the usual trade-off: SaaS convenience with vendor lock-in, or open-source complexity with fragile plugin stacks.

## The opportunity

The CMS market is large and growing, but most solutions either **tax content with recurring SaaS fees** or **burden operators with databases, npm chains, and security debt**. Agencies, privacy-conscious SMBs, and API-first developers need a system that is **simple to run** (upload PHP, no database), **simple to extend** (marketplace + auto-discovered plugins), and **simple to own** (portable JSON/Markdown files).

## Our solution

Birkly delivers:

- **Zero-dependency PHP core** — production without npm, composer, or database
- **Modern admin + Public API** — editors and developers share one content layer
- **Studio** — in-CMS visual builder and IDE (shipped P22–P25)
- **Commerce & Events** — first-party plugins for real business sites
- **Birkly Marketplace** — Stripe Connect, licenses, QC’d catalog (P9–P10 shipped)

Reference sites (Birkly-Website, kekaba) and synced documentation demonstrate a full platform, not a prototype.
