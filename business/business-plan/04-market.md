# 04 — Market

## Market definition

Birkly competes at the intersection of:

1. **Self-hosted / open CMS** — teams that want to run software on their infrastructure.
2. **Headless / API-first content** — developers integrating content into custom frontends.
3. **SMB digital presence** — business sites, blogs, landing pages, light commerce.

### SAM — Self-hosted & developer-led SMB segment

Subset where Birkly’s values resonate:

- Agencies and freelancers building **5–50 sites/year**
- SMBs with **technical advisor** or internal dev
- EU and privacy-conscious buyers favoring **data residency**
- Teams rejecting **per-seat SaaS** scaling

**Assumption:** ~**5–8%** of TAM ≈ **$750M–$2B** SAM for “own your stack” CMS + extensions.

## Customer segments (priority order)

1. **Digital agencies** — standardized stack, marketplace plugins, client handoff.
2. **Product-minded developers** — side projects, startups, internal tools.
3. **SMB owners** — with agency or hosted Birkly partner.
4. **Marketplace providers** — theme/plugin authors monetizing on Birkly Marketplace.

## Market trends supporting timing

- **SaaS fatigue** — subscription stacking on SMB budgets.
- **AI content workflows** — demand for structured, approvable content APIs.
- **Supply-chain security** — preference for auditable, minimal-dependency runtimes.
- **EU data sovereignty** — self-hosting as compliance strategy, not ideology.
